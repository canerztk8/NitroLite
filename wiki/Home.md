# NitroLite wiki

NitroLite is a small, open-source replacement for NitroSense on Acer Nitro and Predator laptops. The
**[README](https://github.com/canerztk8/NitroLite#readme)** is the short version; this wiki has the details.

**[Download](https://github.com/canerztk8/NitroLite/releases/latest)** ·
**[Report a problem](https://github.com/canerztk8/NitroLite/issues)** ·
**[Support the project](https://ko-fi.com/cztrk)**

## Start here

- **[Features](https://github.com/canerztk8/NitroLite/wiki/Features)**: everything NitroLite does, page by page.
- **[Installing on Windows](https://github.com/canerztk8/NitroLite/wiki/Installing-on-Windows)**: Scoop or the zip,
  checking the download, when it asks for administrator rights, uninstalling.
- **[Linux](https://github.com/canerztk8/NitroLite/wiki/Linux)**: install, the one-button setup, what works on which
  kernel, and how to unlock more.
- **[Why SmartScreen warns](https://github.com/canerztk8/NitroLite/wiki/Why-SmartScreen-warns)**: what the "Windows
  protected your PC" prompt means and how to check the file yourself.
